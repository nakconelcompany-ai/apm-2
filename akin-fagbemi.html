About
Profile

Akin Fagbemi is a dynamic and forward-thinking leader from Oyo State with a strong record of community service, youth mobilisation, and civic engagement. He has dedicated his professional life to bridging the gap between government and the governed — ensuring that the concerns of ordinary people find expression in the corridors of power.

With a background in community development and public administration, Fagbemi brings both technical competence and genuine passion for the welfare of his constituents. He has been at the forefront of several community-led initiatives that have improved livelihoods, created opportunities, and deepened civic participation in his federal constituency.

As the APM 2027 House of Representatives candidate, he represents a new generation of Nigerian lawmakers — tech-savvy, transparent, accountable, and deeply connected to the people they serve.

"I am going to Abuja to deliver — roads, healthcare, education and real opportunities for every community in Ibadan North-West and Ibadan South-West. Not promises, but results."
— Mogaji (Dr.) Akin Stephen Fagbemi
Track Record
Public Service Record
  • Traffic Management & Road Safety (OYRTMA): Led the administration of Oyo State's road transport and traffic management operations, implementing road safety initiatives across the state as Executive Chairman.

  • Youth Empowerment: Championed youth development, skills acquisition, entrepreneurship and opportunities for young people in his community.

  • Pinnacle Performer Award 2026: Recognised by Radio Nigeria's Ibadan Zonal Station with the Pinnacle Performer of the Year Award for outstanding contributions to public service.
2027 Vision
Representation Agenda for Ibadan North-West / South-West

Akin Fagbemi's legislative agenda centres on three priorities that his constituency has demanded for years: infrastructure, youth employment, and educational investment.

In the House of Representatives, he will fight aggressively for constituency development fund projects that deliver real, visible results — roads, schools, health centres, water, and power — not phantom projects that exist only on paper.

He will sponsor bills promoting digital economy inclusion, youth entrepreneurship support, and mandatory federal transparency in constituency project disbursement. His lawmaking will always be guided by one question: does this improve the daily life of the people who sent me here?
