About
Profile

Bimbola Adekanmbi is a seasoned politician and community leader with deep roots across the local government areas of Oyo State. With a career spanning public administration, grassroots advocacy, and party organisation, he has earned a reputation as a diligent, accessible, and principled public figure.

His political journey has been shaped by a consistent focus on the welfare of ordinary people — market women, smallholder farmers, artisans, and youth — the constituencies that are too often overlooked in Nigerian governance. He has served in various party and government advisory roles, building practical experience in policy implementation and stakeholder engagement.

As the APM 2027 governorship candidate for Oyo State, Adekanmbi represents the next chapter of people-centred leadership — building on foundations already laid and pushing further to reach every ward and every household.

"Oyo State is blessed with land, people, and potential. What we need is a government that shows up — in every community, for every citizen, every single day."
— Bimbola Adekanmbi

2027 Vision
Governorship Aspiration for Oyo State

Bimbola Adekanmbi's vision for Oyo State as governor is anchored in three pillars: continuity of the transformative programmes already delivering results, expansion of those programmes to reach every ward and LGA, and innovation in attracting new investment and economic opportunity to the state.

He intends to deepen investment in the agricultural value chain, create a robust skills economy for the youth, and ensure that every community — urban and rural — benefits from modern infrastructure, reliable power, and accessible healthcare and education.

Above all, his governorship will be defined by transparency, accountability, and a government that listens first.
