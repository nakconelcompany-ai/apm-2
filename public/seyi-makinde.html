About
Profile

Engineer Oluseyi Abiodun Makinde was born on 25 December 1967 in Ibadan, Oyo State. He studied Electrical Engineering at the University of Lagos, graduating in 1990, and went on to build a career in the oil and gas industry.

In 1997, he established Makon Engineering and Technical Services Limited, growing it into a successful indigenous engineering firm. He became Governor of Oyo State in 2019 and was re-elected in 2023, bringing real executive governance experience to the national stage as the APM presidential candidate for 2027.

As a member of the Allied Peoples' Movement (APM), Makinde's presidential candidacy draws on his background in engineering, business and state governance to address Nigeria's most pressing national challenges.

Track Record
Key Achievements & Governing Record
  • Agriculture & Economic Development: Launched the Fasola Agribusiness Transformation Centre and the Eruwa agribusiness project, supporting agricultural production, processing and economic activity across the state.
  • Infrastructure & Electricity: Delivered road rehabilitation projects and an 11 MW independent power project serving the Oyo State Secretariat and surrounding communities.
  • Healthcare: Implemented the Omituntun Free Health Mission, improved primary healthcare facilities and recruited additional healthcare workers across the state.
  • Education: Introduced education programmes and student support initiatives to improve access to learning and upgrade school infrastructure.
  • Security: Supported security operations including Operation Amotekun and provided security patrol vehicles to protect communities across Oyo State.
2027 Vision
Vision & Aspirations for Nigeria

Makinde's proposed Reset Nigeria Agenda identifies economic reform, education, national security, and governance as its core pillars. His vision is to translate the progress made in Oyo State into a national model of accountable, people-centred leadership.

Economic Reform & Job Creation: Promote productive industries, reduce avoidable business costs and create conditions that support investment and employment.

Education & Skills: Improve educational standards, teacher development and technical training to prepare young Nigerians for employment and entrepreneurship.

National Security: Strengthen intelligence, early-warning systems and coordination among security agencies, including potential reforms to security responsibilities.

Governance & Accountability: Strengthen public institutions, improve transparency and promote more effective coordination between federal, state and local governments.

Food Security: Support agricultural production, food processing, storage and distribution to improve food availability across Nigeria.

National Unity: Promote inclusive governance and national dialogue, including a proposed four-year transitional Government of National Unity if elected.
